STAR Recorder

Record your screen or camera.

A small Chrome extension that captures what's on your screen — or your webcam — and saves the video straight to your computer. No uploads, no watermark, no time limit.

How to install

Free · Chrome on Mac & Windows

What it does

Screen or camera

Capture a whole screen, a single window, one browser tab, or your webcam.

Your microphone too

Narrate while you record, or leave the mic off for a silent capture.

Saved to your computer

Videos download straight to you. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Installing it

STAR Recorder isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet, so it's installed manually. It takes about a minute.

  1. Get the extension folder and put it somewhere you'll remember.
  2. Open Chrome and go to chrome://extensions
  3. Switch on Developer mode (top-right corner).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the folder.
  5. Click the STAR Recorder icon, make an account, and hit record.

On a Mac, the first recording may need permission at System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording. Switch on Chrome, then restart it.

STAR Recorder is in early development — expect rough edges.
